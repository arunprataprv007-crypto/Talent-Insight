Act as a senior Full Stack Software Architect, UI/UX Designer, Product Manager, AI Engineer, and LegalTech SaaS expert.

Your task is to design and build a production-ready, enterprise-grade Full Stack Legal AI Platform for India.

Project Name:
NyayaAI (Working Title)

Tagline:
"India's AI-Powered Legal Intelligence Platform"

The application should be modern, scalable, secure, responsive, and AI-first.

======================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================

Build a complete LegalTech platform that enables Indian citizens, advocates, law firms, businesses, and students to:

• View live legal statistics
• Search court cases
• Understand Indian laws under BNS, BNSS, and BSA
• Track case status
• Upload legal documents for AI analysis
• Generate legal drafts
• Chat with AI using OpenAI GPT and Anthropic Claude
• Book consultations with advocates
• Learn Indian law through AI
• Access legal news and judgments
• Manage legal documents securely

The application should be designed as a SaaS platform with role-based access.
